# Privacy policy

**Try-On for Steam Profiles** (the browser extension and the userscript)
collects no data about you. Last updated: 6 November 2026.

- **Nothing is collected or sent to the developer.** There are no analytics,
  no tracking, no ads, no accounts and no servers.
- **What's stored stays in your browser.** Your presets, settings, panel
  position and the look you're trying on are kept in your browser's local
  storage for the extension (or Tampermonkey's, for the userscript). They
  never leave your browser. Uninstalling removes them, and so does
  **Advanced → Erase everything**.
- **It only talks to Steam.** To read item details from the pages you're
  previewing, it requests pages from `steamcommunity.com` and
  `store.steampowered.com`: your mini profile, the preview of a Game Profile
  bundle, and a game's name. Like Steam's own pages, these requests carry
  your Steam login cookies, so Steam sees them as you browsing Steam. Item
  images and videos load from Steam's own servers, as on any Steam page.
- **"Report a problem" only runs when you click it.** It opens GitHub's
  new-issue form in a new tab, filled in with what the panel sees on the
  page, with your profile address, SteamID, login, display name and uploaded
  avatar removed. Nothing is sent unless you choose to submit that issue
  yourself, and GitHub's privacy policy applies to what you submit.

Not affiliated with Valve. Questions:
[open an issue](https://github.com/brunosuzano/try-on-for-steam-profiles/issues).
Changes to this policy appear in this file's history.
